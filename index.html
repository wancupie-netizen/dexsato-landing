The Crypto Market Does Not Have an Information Problem.
It Has an Attention Problem.

Every day, traders are exposed to thousands of charts, alerts, indicators, posts, rumours, narratives, and trading signals. More information does not automatically create better decisions. It often creates hesitation, distraction, and noise.

◫

Too Many Signals

Most scanners generate more alerts than any trader can realistically evaluate.

⇄

Conflicting Opinions

Social media, influencers, technical indicators, and market narratives frequently point in different directions.

◎

No Clear Judgement

Raw data may show what happened, but it rarely explains why the situation deserves attention.

◷

Constant Monitoring

Traders spend valuable time repeatedly checking markets that may not require any action.
